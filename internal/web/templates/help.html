Help & file handling

Upload your images, audio, or video, review the detected files and selected outputs, then choose Convert. Download successful results individually, together as a ZIP, or as separate browser downloads. Your browser may ask you to allow multiple downloads for this site.

Keep your originals. These presets create converted copies, not archival backups. Check important results before sharing them.

Shared workspace

Everyone who can access Filetwist can view, download, cancel, and delete every job. There are no private user accounts. Authentication at a reverse proxy limits access to the workspace; it does not separate users' files.

Uploads and results expire after {{.RetentionLabel}}. Active work and downloads can postpone cleanup. Cancel stops conversion but keeps stored files. Delete removes the job, its originals, and its results; retry if a download is still holding the job open.

Image presets strip capture metadata such as EXIF and GPS while retaining supported ICC color profiles. Audio and video presets strip container/stream metadata and chapters. This is not an anonymization guarantee: filenames, visible content, and other data can still identify you.

Video presets keep the first usable video stream and first supported audio track. Unsupported audio can be omitted with a warning; subtitles, extra tracks, attachments, and chapters are dropped. Supported PQ/HLG HDR video is converted to SDR; the original HDR presentation and Dolby Vision metadata are not preserved.

Animated or multipage images and HDR still-image tone mapping are unsupported. PNG and FLAC cannot recover information already lost in the source.

Work with several files

Open "Apply a preset to several files" during review to change eligible files together. "Keep individual choices" preserves presets you changed separately. Files that do not support the batch preset stay unchanged.

Remove unwanted files before conversion starts. Removing the last file deletes the empty job. Once a job starts, use Cancel to stop work or Delete job to remove it.

When something goes wrong

If the queue is full or a request fails, your selections stay available so you can retry. An unsupported or failed file reports its own message; other successful results remain downloadable.

After a server restart, unfinished jobs are marked interrupted and do not resume. Upload your originals again to retry a failed, canceled, or interrupted conversion. A job that has expired or been deleted cannot be recovered here.

For self-hosting users

The local CLI uses the same conversion profiles, one file at a time. Unlike the web form, it rejects an unavailable default rather than recommending a different operation; choose an eligible alternative explicitly.

See the CLI guide, deployment guide, and full profile specifications.
